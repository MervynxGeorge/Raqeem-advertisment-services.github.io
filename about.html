Our journey

Built in Dubai. Made for everywhere.

Raqeem began with a question: what if every brand had access to the same strategic rigour and creative firepower as the world's most admired companies — without the layers of a traditional agency?

We set out to build a different kind of partner. One that combines sharp strategic thinking, high-craft creative and purposeful media planning under one roof. A partner that cares as much about your quarterly numbers as it does about the beauty of the idea.

Today, Raqeem works with startups, scale-ups and established enterprises across the Middle East and beyond — helping each one find their point of view, earn attention, and build momentum that lasts.
